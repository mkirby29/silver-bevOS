Glossary

BevOS: Workflow coordination software platform being developed for Nice Package Co. Brand: The intangible sum of a company’s reputation, values, and identity.

Client: Organization or individuals purchasing the service or products of another member of the overall manufacturing industry as part of their own business operations pipeline.

Co-Packers: Contract packager, a company that packages products for clients as a service to cover the enclosing and protecting products for distribution, storage, sale, and use.

Enterprise Resource Planning: A software system that integrates and manages core business processes such as finance, inventory, manufacturing, sales, and HR all with one platform. ERP provides a centralized database for unified workflow that allows multiple people to share information across departments.

Nice Package Company: Industrial coordination company working between internal customers to assist them in coordinating the beverage manufacturing and distribution process. Client for development of BevOS.
