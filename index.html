Elevator Pitch

The beverage packaging industry was estimated to be at USD 157.73 billion in 2023 and is projected to reach USD 222.08 billion by 2030 (Grand View Research, 2024.) Beverage manufacturers often manage product launches through disconnected spreadsheets, email chains, and multiple software systems. This fragmented approach can lead to delays, communication breakdowns, and costly operational errors. BevOS is a centralized workflow management platform designed specifically for beverage manufacturing. The platform connects suppliers, co-packers, logistics providers, and operational teams into a single system, providing visibility across the entire production lifecycle. By streamlining approvals, tracking production activities, and coordinating logistics, BevOS helps companies operate more efficiently and scale their business with fewer operational bottlenecks.

Problem Description

As beverage companies grow, coordination of production activities become more challenging to manage. The beverage manufacturing industry relies on multiple spreadsheets, email chains, and phone calls. While these are all helpful tools that allow companies to operate they create inefficiencies as a business grows. Important updates can become lost in long email chains, causing delays and miscommunication between brands, suppliers, co-packers, and logistic providers. Approval processes for artwork, purchase orders, and production schedules frequently require a manual follow up creating bottlenecks that slow projects and increase workload. BevOS addresses these challenges by providing a centralized workflow management platform specifically designed for beverage manufacturing operations.

Customer and End User

BevOS is designed for beverage manugacturing companies of all sizes, including startups, contract manufacturers, and established beverage brands. Primary users include production managers, operations teams, quality assurance personnel, supply chain coordinators, logistics managers, and company executives who need accurate, real-time operational data.

Solution Statement

Current beverage manufacturing operations rely heavily on manual coordination between suppliers, production facilities, logistics providers, and finance teams. These processes are often managed through spreadsheets, emails, and disconnected software applications, creating inefficiencies and increasing the risk of errors. BevOS addresses this problem by providing a unified operational platform that centralizes workflow management, approval tracking, production scheduling, document management, and logistics coordination. The goal is to imporve visibility, reduce manual effort, and create a scalable system that supports business growth.
